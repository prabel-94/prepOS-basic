MSMDF-LX v1.1 is a multilingual cognitive reconstruction architecture designed to regenerate representational cognition from MSMDF canonical exports across languages while preserving chronology cognition, retrieval cognition, narrative continuity, structural hierarchy, semantic anchors, representation specialization, and cognitive compression behavior.
