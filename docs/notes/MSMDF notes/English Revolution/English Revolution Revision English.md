# English Revolution (1603–1689)

# One-Line Revision

The English Revolution (1603–1689) transformed England from a monarchy claiming **Divine Right** into a **Constitutional Monarchy** founded upon **Parliamentary Sovereignty** and the **Rule of Law**.

# 12. Final Revision Sentence

**The English Revolution was the culmination of centuries of constitutional evolution that permanently transferred political supremacy from the Crown to Parliament, established the Rule of Law, created Constitutional Monarchy, and laid the intellectual and constitutional foundations of modern liberal democracy.**
