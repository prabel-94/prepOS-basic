1. Which document first established the principle that the monarch was subject to law?
   → [[Magna Carta]]

   → [[Henry VIII]]

   → [[James I]]

   → [[Petition of Right]]

   → [[Ship Money]]

   → [[William Laud]]

7. Which period is known as the "Eleven Years' Tyranny"?
   → [[Personal Rule]]

8. Which Parliament issued the [[Grand Remonstrance]]?
   → [[Long Parliament]]

9. Which battle became the decisive parliamentary victory?
   → [[Battle of Naseby]]

    → [[New Model Army]]

11. Which event challenged the sacred foundations of monarchy?
    → [[Execution of Charles I]]

12. Which republican government replaced the monarchy?
    → [[Commonwealth]]

13. Which written constitution established the [[Protectorate]]?
    → [[Instrument of Government]]

    → [[Declaration of Breda]]

    → [[Habeas Corpus Act]]

    → [[Exclusion Crisis]]

    → [[William of Orange]]

    → [[Glorious Revolution]]

# 3. Relationship Recall

1. Why was [[Ship Money]] controversial?
   → It was imposed without parliamentary consent.

2. Why did [[Charles I]] recall Parliament in 1640?
   → Financial crisis caused by the [[Bishops' Wars]].

3. Why was the [[New Model Army]] historically significant?
   → It became a disciplined professional army that secured parliamentary victory.

4. Why are the [[Putney Debates]] important?
   → They debated popular sovereignty and representative government.

5. Why is the [[Bill of Rights]] considered a constitutional milestone?
   → It permanently limited royal authority and strengthened Parliament.
